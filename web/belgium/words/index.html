Dutch and French office words from Belgium, explained

The words you see in official letters in Belgium, in plain English.

aangetekende brief
Registered letter. You get proof that you sent it.
aanmaning
Payment reminder.
aanslagbiljet
Tax bill. It shows how much income tax or property tax you must pay, or get back, for one year.
aanslagjaar
Tax year. Aanslagjaar 2026 is about the income you earned in 2025.
afbetalingsplan
Payment plan to pay a debt in monthly parts.
allocations de chômage
Unemployment benefit, decided by the ONEM and paid by your union or the CAPAC.
amende administrative (SAC)
Fine from the town hall, for example for parking, litter or noise.
arbeidsrechtbank
Labour court. You can appeal social security decisions there, usually within 3 months and for free.
avertissement-extrait de rôle
Tax bill. It shows how much income tax or property tax you must pay, or get back, for one year.
beslag
Seizure of money or goods to pay a debt, for example part of your wage.
bevel om het grondgebied te verlaten
Order to leave Belgium by a set date.
bevel tot betalen
Formal order from a bailiff to pay a debt. After it, your goods or money can be seized.
bezwaarschrift
Written objection against a tax bill. You can send it online in MyMinfin or by post.
citation
Summons to appear in court on a set date.
commandement de payer
Formal order from a bailiff to pay a debt. After it, your goods or money can be seized.
communication structurée
Payment code like +++123/4567/89002+++. It links your payment to your file.
CPAS
Public social welfare centre in your town. It helps with money, debt and housing problems.
dagvaarding
Summons to appear in court on a set date.
endéans
Belgian French for “within”, as in “endéans les 15 jours” — within 15 days.
exercice d’imposition
Tax year. Exercice d’imposition 2026 is about the income you earned in 2025.
GAS-boete
Fine from the town hall, for example for parking, litter or noise.
gerechtsdeurwaarder
Bailiff. A legal officer who delivers court papers and collects debts.
gestructureerde mededeling
Payment code like +++123/4567/89002+++. It links your payment to your file.
Groeipakket
Child benefit in Flanders.
huissier de justice
Bailiff. A legal officer who delivers court papers and collects debts.
huurwaarborg
Rental deposit, usually on a blocked bank account in your name.
indu
Money paid to you by mistake that they want back.
ingebrekestelling
Formal demand to pay or act. The last step before court.
justice de paix
Local court for rent, small debts and neighbour problems.
leefloon
Living wage from the OCMW for people with no or too little income.
mise en demeure
Formal demand to pay or act. The last step before court.
mutualité
Health insurance fund. It pays back medical costs and pays sickness benefits.
numéro de registre national
National register number. Your personal number in Belgium, also on your identity card.
OCMW
Public social welfare centre in your town. It helps with money, debt and housing problems.
onmiddellijke inning
Offer to pay a traffic fine quickly and end the case.
onroerende voorheffing
Yearly property tax for the owner of a house or flat.
ordre de quitter le territoire
Order to leave Belgium by a set date.
perception immédiate
Offer to pay a traffic fine quickly and end the case.
personenbelasting
Personal income tax.
plan de paiement
Payment plan to pay a debt in monthly parts.
précompte immobilier
Yearly property tax for the owner of a house or flat.
réclamation
Written objection against a tax bill. You can send it online in MyMinfin or by post.
revenu d’intégration
Living wage from the CPAS for people with no or too little income.
rijksregisternummer
National register number. Your personal number in Belgium, also on your identity card.
saisie
Seizure of money or goods to pay a debt, for example part of your wage.
terugvordering
Request to pay back money you got but weren’t entitled to.
tribunal du travail
Labour court. You can appeal social security decisions there, usually within 3 months and for free.
vredegerecht
Local court for rent, small debts and neighbour problems.
werkloosheidsuitkering
Unemployment benefit, decided by the RVA and paid by your union or the HVW.
ziekenfonds
Health insurance fund. It pays back medical costs and pays sickness benefits.
