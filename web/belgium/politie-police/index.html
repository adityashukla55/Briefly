Letters you may get

  • Voorstel tot onmiddellijke inning / perception immédiate — a traffic fine to pay by the date in the letter
  • Minnelijke schikking / transaction — a higher offer from the prosecutor after an unpaid fine
  • Vraag om de bestuurder te identificeren / demande d’identification du conducteur — say who was driving

What to do

  • If you weren’t the driver, say who was driving by the date in the letter (usually within 15 days).
  • If you don’t pay or answer, the fine usually goes up or you are summoned to the police court.
  • Find the deadline. Look for “termijn”, “uiterlijk op”, “délai”, “au plus tard le”.
  • Write the reference number from the letter on everything you send back, and keep copies.
  • If you disagree with a decision, object in writing before the deadline (“bezwaarschrift”, “beroep”, “verzet”, “bezwaar”). See the template.

Questions

What is the Politie / Police — Parket / Parquet?

Police and traffic fines. Sends traffic fines and other police letters. Unpaid fines go to the public prosecutor (parket / parquet).

How long do I have to answer a letter from the Politie / Police — Parket / Parquet?

It depends on the letter. Look for words like “termijn”, “uiterlijk op”, “délai”, “au plus tard le” — they mark the deadline. In Belgium, a letter sent by post is usually treated as received on the third working day after the date it was sent. Many deadlines, for example 6 months to object to a tax bill, start from that day. To object, the letter usually names the way to do it (“bezwaarschrift”, “beroep”, “verzet”, “bezwaar”).
