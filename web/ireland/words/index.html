English office words from Ireland, explained

The words you see in official letters in Ireland, in plain English.

appearance
A form that tells the court you will defend the case.
arrears
Money you owe because regular payments were missed, for example rent.
attachment of earnings
A court order that takes money for a debt or fine straight from your pay or social welfare.
Child Benefit
A monthly payment for each child, paid to parents.
Citizens Information
A free public service with information and advice on rights, social welfare, housing and more.
civil bill
Court papers that start a case in the Circuit Court.
civil summons
Court papers that start a case in the District Court, for example for a debt.
deciding officer
The person in social welfare who made the decision on your claim.
fixed charge notice
A fine for a road offence. Pay within 28 days, or 50% more within the next 28 days.
HAP
Housing Assistance Payment. The council pays most of your rent to the landlord; you pay a rent contribution to the council.
Intreo
The local office of the Department of Social Protection for jobseekers and social welfare.
IRP
Irish Residence Permit. The card that shows you are registered and allowed to stay in Ireland.
Jobseeker’s Allowance
A weekly payment for people out of work. Jobseeker’s Benefit depends on your PRSI; Jobseeker’s Allowance depends on your means.
judgment
The court’s decision. A judgment for a debt can go on the public Register of Judgments.
Local Property Tax
A yearly tax on homes, paid to Revenue by the owner.
MABS
Money Advice and Budgeting Service. Free, confidential help with debt and budgeting.
means test
A check of your income and savings to see if you qualify for a payment.
medical card
A card from the HSE that gives free GP visits and many health services to people with low income.
motor tax
Tax you must pay to use a car on public roads. Pay at motortax.ie.
MPRN
Meter Point Reference Number. The number of your electricity meter.
myAccount
Your free online account with Revenue, where you see letters, tax credits and refunds.
MyWelfare
The online service of the Department of Social Protection, where you can apply, see letters and appeal.
NCT
National Car Test. A regular safety test for cars older than 4 years.
notice of termination
A written notice from your landlord or you to end a tenancy. It must follow strict rules.
overpayment
Money paid to you by mistake that they want back.
penalty points
Points on your driving licence for road offences. 12 points (7 for learners and new drivers) means you lose your licence for 6 months.
penalty rate
A lower jobseeker’s payment, for example after missing a meeting without a good reason.
phased payment arrangement
A plan with Revenue to pay tax you owe in parts.
PPS number
Personal Public Service Number. Your personal number for tax, social welfare and public services.
PRSI
Pay Related Social Insurance. Contributions from your pay that give you the right to some social welfare payments and the State Pension.
rent review
A change of your rent. In many areas, increases are limited by law.
Revenue
The Irish tax office.
review
A new look at a decision, or a check that you still qualify for a payment.
ROS
Revenue Online Service. Used by the self-employed and businesses to file tax returns.
RTB
Residential Tenancies Board. It registers tenancies and settles disputes between tenants and landlords.
sheriff
An official who collects debts after a judgment, or for Revenue. Fees are added to the debt.
Social Welfare Appeals Office
An independent office that looks at appeals against social welfare decisions.
Statement of Liability
A yearly summary from Revenue of your income and tax. It shows a refund or an underpayment.
statute-barred
A debt too old to take to court — usually 6 years after the last payment or written acknowledgement.
Tax Appeals Commission
An independent body that hears appeals against Revenue decisions. You usually must appeal within 30 days.
tax credits
Amounts that lower the tax you pay. Your employer uses them from your tax credit certificate.
TV licence
A yearly licence you need if you have a TV set at home. Paid to An Post.
underpayment
Tax you didn’t pay in full. Revenue often collects it by lowering your tax credits over a few years.
USC
Universal Social Charge. A tax on income, on top of income tax.
