Letters you may get

  • Rozliczenie mediów — the bill for heating, water and other costs
  • Wezwanie do zapłaty — unpaid rent or fees
  • Wypowiedzenie umowy najmu — notice to end your rental contract
  • Zmiana wysokości opłat — your monthly fees change

What to do

  • The owner must return your deposit (kaucja) within 1 month after you move out, minus any money you still owe.
  • With an occasional lease (najem okazjonalny), you signed a notary statement that you will move out when asked. Take every notice seriously and get advice.
  • Find the deadline. Look for “termin”, “do dnia”, “od dnia doręczenia”.
  • Write the reference number from the letter on everything you send back, and keep copies.
  • If you disagree with a decision, object in writing before the deadline (“odwołanie”, “zażalenie”, “sprzeciw”, “skarga”, “reklamacja”). See the template.

Words you may see

wezwanie
Official request. They want you to do something — come in, send papers or pay — by a set date.
kaucja
Deposit, usually for a rented flat. The owner must return it within 1 month after you move out, minus money you still owe.
wypowiedzenie
Notice to end a contract, for example for a flat, a job or a phone contract.
najem okazjonalny
Occasional lease. A rental contract with a notary statement that makes eviction faster.

Questions

What is the Wynajmujący / wspólnota / spółdzielnia mieszkaniowa?

Landlord or housing association. The owner of your flat, or the association or cooperative that runs your building.

How long do I have to answer a letter from the Wynajmujący / wspólnota / spółdzielnia mieszkaniowa?

It depends on the letter. Look for words like “termin”, “do dnia”, “od dnia doręczenia” — they mark the deadline. In Poland, most deadlines start on the day you receive the letter (doręczenie). If you weren’t home, the post leaves a notice (awizo), and a letter you don’t collect usually counts as delivered 14 days after the first notice. The 3 days are only an estimate, so check the date you signed for it. To object, the letter usually names the way to do it (“odwołanie”, “zażalenie”, “sprzeciw”, “skarga”, “reklamacja”).
