Polish office words from Poland, explained

The words you see in official letters in Poland, in plain English.

awizo
Notice from the post office that a registered letter is waiting for you.
czynsz
Rent. It can also mean the monthly fee to the housing association or cooperative.
decyzja
Official decision by an office. It becomes final if you don’t appeal in time.
doręczenie
Delivery — the day you received the letter. Most deadlines start from this day.
e-Doręczenia
Electronic registered mail for official letters. A letter sent there counts like a registered letter on paper.
e-Sąd
Online court for payment orders (elektroniczne postępowanie upominawcze, EPU). Debt collectors use it a lot.
e-Urząd Skarbowy
The tax office’s online service on podatki.gov.pl. You can file returns, pay and write to the office there.
egzekucja
Enforcement. Collecting a debt by force, for example from your bank account or wages.
ePUAP
Online platform for writing to Polish offices. You log in with a profil zaufany.
grzywna
Fine. Money you pay as a penalty.
karta pobytu
Residence card. The plastic card that shows your residence permit.
kaucja
Deposit, usually for a rented flat. The owner must return it within 1 month after you move out, minus money you still owe.
komornik
Court bailiff. Collects debts by force for a creditor, based on a court order.
mandat
Fine for a minor offence, for example from the police or the city guard.
meldunek
Registering your address at the town or city office.
najem okazjonalny
Occasional lease. A rental contract with a notary statement that makes eviction faster.
nakaz zapłaty
Court payment order. The court orders you to pay without a hearing first.
nieodpłatna pomoc prawna
Free legal help at local advice points. Anyone who can’t afford a lawyer can use it.
NIP
Tax ID number. If you don’t run a business, you usually use your PESEL instead.
odsetki
Interest. Extra money added because you paid late.
odwołanie
Appeal against a decision. You usually send it through the office that made the decision.
okres wypowiedzenia
Notice period. The time between the notice and the end of the contract.
opłata skarbowa
Stamp duty. A fee for some official documents and permits, paid to the town or city.
pełnomocnictwo
Power of attorney. A signed paper that lets someone else act for you, for example a lawyer or a friend.
PESEL
Your 11-digit Polish personal ID number. Offices use it to find your file.
PIT
Personal income tax, and the yearly tax return forms (PIT-37, PIT-36…).
postanowienie
Ruling on one step in a case, not the final decision. Some rulings can be challenged with a complaint (zażalenie).
pouczenie
Instructions at the end of a letter. They explain your rights: if you can appeal, where, and by when.
profil zaufany
Free online ID for Polish public services. You use it to log in and sign forms online.
przedawnienie
Expiry of a claim after a time limit, often 3 or 6 years. After that, a court usually can’t make a consumer pay.
przywrócenie terminu
Reopening a deadline you missed through no fault of your own, for example because you were in hospital.
rozłożenie na raty
Paying a debt in instalments.
skarga
Complaint. For example to an administrative court (sąd administracyjny), or about what a bailiff did (skarga na czynności komornika).
składka
Contribution, for example for social or health insurance.
sprzeciw
Objection, for example to a court payment order (nakaz zapłaty). If you send it in time, the order stops and the court looks at the case properly.
sygnatura akt
Court case number. Write it on every letter to the court or bailiff.
termin
Deadline or date. “W terminie 14 dni” means “within 14 days”.
tytuł wykonawczy
The official paper that allows a debt to be collected by force.
umorzenie
Cancelling a debt, or closing a case. Check which one your letter means.
upomnienie
Official reminder to pay. If you don’t pay within 7 days of delivery, enforcement can start.
Urząd Skarbowy
Tax office (US). It handles income tax and other state taxes.
uzasadnienie
The reasons for a decision. Read it to see what to argue in an appeal.
wezwanie
Official request. They want you to do something — come in, send papers or pay — by a set date.
wypowiedzenie
Notice to end a contract, for example for a flat, a job or a phone contract.
zajęcie
Seizure. Money is taken from your bank account, wages or benefits to pay a debt.
zaległość
Arrears. Money you should have paid already.
zażalenie
Complaint against a ruling (postanowienie). The deadline is usually 7 days from delivery.
zezwolenie na pobyt
Residence permit, for example temporary (czasowy) or permanent (stały).
znak sprawy
Case reference of an office. Write it on every reply.
ZUS
Social Insurance Institution. It collects contributions and pays pensions, sick pay and 800+.
