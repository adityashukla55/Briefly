What does “Betreibung” mean?

Debt collection through the state. Anyone who says you owe them money can start one — without proof.

What to do

Never ignore it. A Betreibung shows up in the register that landlords check, even if you don’t owe the money.

You may see it in letters from

  • Betreibungsamt — Debt collection office
  • Inkassobüro — Debt collection agency
