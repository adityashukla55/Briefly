German office words from Switzerland, explained

The words you see in official letters in Switzerland, in plain English.

Abholungseinladung
A note from the post that a letter is waiting for you. You have 7 days to pick it up.
AHV
The Swiss state pension (old-age and survivors’ insurance). Almost everyone who lives or works in Switzerland pays into it.
Arbeitsbemühungen
Your job applications. You must show them to the RAV every month, by the 5th of the next month.
Arbeitslosenkasse
Unemployment fund. It pays your unemployment money.
Ausländerausweis
Permit card for foreigners. L = short stay, B = residence, C = settlement, G = cross-border worker.
Beschwerde
Appeal or complaint to a higher office or a court.
Betreibung
Debt collection through the state. Anyone who says you owe them money can start one — without proof.
Betreibungsamt
Debt collection office. A state office for your area.
Betreibungsregisterauszug
Extract from the debt collection register. Landlords and employers often ask for it.
Einschreiben
Registered letter. You sign when you get it.
Einsprache
Objection. You ask the same office to look at its decision again.
Einstelltage
Days without unemployment money, as a penalty — for example after a missed appointment.
Einwohnerkontrolle
Residents’ office of your town. You register here within 14 days when you move in, and tell them when you move out.
Franchise
Deductible. The part of your medical costs you pay yourself each year before the insurance pays. A higher Franchise means a lower premium.
Frist
Deadline.
Gemeinde
Your town or village (municipality).
Gläubiger
Creditor. The person or company that says you owe them money.
Grundversicherung
Basic health insurance. It’s required by law for everyone who lives in Switzerland.
innert
Within. “Innert 10 Tagen” means within 10 days.
IV
Disability insurance. It is run together with the AHV.
Kanton
One of the 26 states of Switzerland. Taxes and many rules differ by canton.
Kündigung
Notice that ends a contract.
Mahnung
Payment reminder. You paid late or not at all.
Mietzinsdepot
Rental deposit (Kaution). At most 3 months’ rent, kept in a bank account in your name.
Nebenkosten
Extra costs on top of the rent, like heating and hot water.
Ordnungsbusse
Fixed fine for a small offence, for example parking. It doesn’t go on your criminal record.
Pfändung
Seizure. The Betreibungsamt takes part of your wages or things to pay a debt.
Prämie
Premium. The amount you pay for insurance, usually every month.
Prämienverbilligung
Money from your canton to help pay health insurance if your income is low. In many cantons you must apply yourself.
QR-Rechnung
Swiss bill with a payment part and a QR code. Scan the code with your banking app to pay.
Quellensteuer
Tax taken directly from your salary. Common for people with a B or L permit.
Ratenzahlung
Paying in instalments.
RAV
Regional employment centre. It helps you find work and checks that you are looking for a job.
Rechtliches Gehör
Your right to give your view before an office decides about you.
Rechtsmittelbelehrung
The part at the end of a decision that tells you how, where and by when you can object.
Rechtsvorschlag
Your objection to a Zahlungsbefehl. It stops the debt collection until a court or office decides.
Rekurs
Appeal. You ask a higher office or a court to change a decision.
Schlichtungsbehörde
Conciliation office for disputes, for example with your landlord. For rent disputes it costs nothing.
Selbstbehalt
Your share of the costs after the Franchise: 10%, up to a yearly limit.
Serafe
The company that collects the radio and TV fee from every household.
Steuererklärung
Tax return. The form where you list your income and wealth. The deadline is usually in spring.
Strafbefehl
Penalty order from the prosecutor or a fines office. If you don’t object, it counts as a final judgment.
Stundung
Postponing a payment.
Taggeld
Daily allowance. Unemployment money is counted per working day.
Veranlagung
Tax assessment. The office’s calculation of your tax, based on your tax return.
Verfügung
Formal decision by an office. It becomes final if you don’t object in time.
Verlustschein
Certificate of loss. It shows a debt that could not be collected. The creditor can try again for up to 20 years.
Vorladung
Summons. You must come to the police, the prosecutor or a court.
Zahlungsbefehl
Payment order in a debt collection. It is not a court decision: nobody has checked yet if the debt is real.
Zustellung
Delivery of the letter to you. Many deadlines start on the day after.
