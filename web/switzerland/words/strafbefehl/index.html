What does “Strafbefehl” mean?

Penalty order from the prosecutor or a fines office. If you don’t object, it counts as a final judgment.

What to do

If you disagree, send an Einsprache in writing within 10 days.

You may see it in letters from

  • Polizei / Statthalteramt / Stadtrichteramt — Police and fines office
