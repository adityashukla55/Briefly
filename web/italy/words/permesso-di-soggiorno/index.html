What does “Permesso di soggiorno” mean?

Residence permit for people who are not EU citizens.

What to do

Apply to renew it before it ends, and keep the post office receipt (ricevuta) with your passport.

You may see it in letters from

  • Questura (Ufficio Immigrazione) — Police immigration office
