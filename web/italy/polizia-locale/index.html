Letters you may get

  • Verbale di contestazione — a fine, for example for parking or speeding
  • Comunicazione dati del conducente — they ask who was driving
  • Accertamento di residenza — a check that you live at your address

What to do

  • For most traffic fines you pay 30% less if you pay within 5 days of receiving it.
  • If the fine takes points off a driving licence, you must say who was driving, usually within 60 days.
  • Find the deadline. Look for “termine”, “entro il”, “scadenza”.
  • Write the reference number from the letter on everything you send back, and keep copies.
  • If you disagree with a decision, object in writing before the deadline (“ricorso”, “opposizione”, “istanza di autotutela”, “reclamo”). See the template.

Questions

What is the Polizia Locale / Municipale?

Local police. The city police. They send traffic and parking fines (verbali) and check that people live at their registered address.

How long do I have to answer a letter from the Polizia Locale / Municipale?

It depends on the letter. Look for words like “termine”, “entro il”, “scadenza” — they mark the deadline. In Italy, deadlines usually run from the day the letter counts as delivered (notifica). For registered mail, that is the day you sign for it — or, if you don’t collect it, a set time after the post office leaves a notice. Our estimate adds 3 days to the date on the letter, so check the real date. To object, the letter usually names the way to do it (“ricorso”, “opposizione”, “istanza di autotutela”, “reclamo”).
