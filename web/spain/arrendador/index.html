Letters you may get

  • Actualización de la renta — your rent changes, usually once a year
  • Preaviso de no renovación — your contract won’t be renewed
  • Requerimiento de pago / burofax — unpaid rent; often the step before an eviction case
  • Liquidación de la fianza — what happens with your deposit when you leave

What to do

  • For a main home, rental law usually lets tenants stay at least 5 years (7 if the landlord is a company), for contracts signed since March 2019.
  • Your deposit (fianza) is one month’s rent. The landlord must return it when you leave, minus proven damage or unpaid bills.
  • Find the deadline. Look for “plazo”, “hasta el”, “días hábiles”.
  • Write the reference number from the letter on everything you send back, and keep copies.
  • If you disagree with a decision, object in writing before the deadline (“recurso de reposición”, “reclamación económico-administrativa”, “recurso de alzada”, “reclamación previa”, “alegaciones”, “oposición”, “recurso”). See the template.

Words you may see

Liquidación
Calculation of an amount you must pay, often a tax assessment.
Requerimiento
Formal request to send documents, give information or pay.
Burofax
A letter sent with proof of content and delivery. It is often the step before a court case.
Fianza
Rental deposit. For a home it is one month’s rent.

Questions

What is the Arrendador / inmobiliaria?

Landlord or letting agency. The owner of your flat, or the agency that manages it.

How long do I have to answer a letter from the Arrendador / inmobiliaria?

It depends on the letter. Look for words like “plazo”, “hasta el”, “días hábiles” — they mark the deadline. In Spain, deadlines usually count from the day after the letter is delivered (notificación). A paper letter is delivered on the day you receive it; an electronic one when you open it, or 10 calendar days after it was made available. We estimate 3 days for post — use the real date if you know it. To object, the letter usually names the way to do it (“recurso de reposición”, “reclamación económico-administrativa”, “recurso de alzada”, “reclamación previa”, “alegaciones”, “oposición”, “recurso”).
