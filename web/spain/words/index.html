Spanish office words from Spain, explained

The words you see in official letters in Spain, in plain English.

Alegaciones
Your written arguments and proof, sent before the office makes its final decision.
Aplazamiento
Permission to pay later.
Apremio
Enforced collection. You didn’t pay in time, so a surcharge is added.
Autónomo
Self-employed person. Autónomos pay a monthly Social Security fee (cuota) based on their income.
Burofax
A letter sent with proof of content and delivery. It is often the step before a court case.
Carta de pago
Payment form. Take it to a bank or use its number to pay online.
Certificado digital
A digital ID on your computer or phone. You use it to sign in and sign documents online.
Cita previa
An appointment you book in advance. Many offices won’t see you without one.
Cl@ve
The government’s login system. You sign in with a PIN, a permanent password or the Cl@ve app.
Cobro indebido
Money you got but weren’t entitled to. They will ask for it back.
CSV
Secure verification code. You can use it online to check that a document is genuine.
Cuota
A set amount to pay, for example the monthly Social Security fee. In tax letters it means the tax amount.
Denuncia
In traffic letters, the report of an offence that starts a fine. It can also mean a police report.
Desistimiento
Your application is treated as withdrawn, for example because you didn’t send missing papers in time.
Días hábiles
Working days. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays don’t count.
Días naturales
Calendar days. Every day counts, including weekends.
DNI
Spanish national identity card and number. Only Spanish citizens have one.
Domiciliación
Direct debit. The money is taken from your bank account automatically.
Embargo
Seizure of money or property to pay a debt, for example from your bank account or wages.
Empadronamiento
Registering your address at the town hall. You need it for healthcare, school and many permits.
Fianza
Rental deposit. For a home it is one month’s rent.
Fraccionamiento
Permission to pay in instalments.
Hacienda
Everyday name for the tax office (Agencia Tributaria).
IBI
Yearly property tax, paid to the town hall by the owner.
IRPF
Personal income tax. The yearly tax return (declaración de la renta) is usually filed from April to June.
IVA
Value-added tax (VAT). The standard rate is 21%.
Justicia gratuita
Free legal aid for people with a low income.
Liquidación
Calculation of an amount you must pay, often a tax assessment.
Modelo
A numbered tax form, for example modelo 100 (income tax) or modelo 303 (VAT).
Multa
Fine.
NIE
Foreigner’s identity number. It starts with X, Y or Z and is also your tax number (NIF).
NIF
Tax identity number. For foreigners it is the same as the NIE.
Notificación
Official delivery of a letter. Most deadlines start the day after.
Padrón
The town hall’s list of residents. A certificado de empadronamiento proves where you live.
Plazo
Deadline or time limit.
Plusvalía
Town hall tax when a property is sold, given away or inherited.
Prestación
Benefit — money from the state, for example unemployment benefit.
Propuesta de liquidación
A draft tax assessment. It isn’t final — you can reply with alegaciones first.
Recargo
Surcharge. Extra money added because you paid late.
Reclamación económico-administrativa
Appeal against a tax decision to a special tax tribunal (TEA). Usually within one month.
Reclamación previa
A first complaint to the office, for example Social Security, that you must file before you can go to court.
Recurso de alzada
Appeal to a higher office. Usually within one month.
Recurso de reposición
Appeal to the same office that made the decision. Usually within one month.
Requerimiento
Formal request to send documents, give information or pay.
Resolución
Official decision.
Sanción
Penalty, often a fine.
Sede electrónica
An office’s official website for online forms, payments and letters.
SEPE
The state employment service. It pays unemployment benefit.
Subsidio
A smaller benefit, for example after your unemployment benefit has ended.
TIE
Foreigner’s identity card with your photo. It proves your residence permit.
