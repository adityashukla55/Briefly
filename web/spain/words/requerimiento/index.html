What does “Requerimiento” mean?

Formal request to send documents, give information or pay.

What to do

Answer in time, even if only to ask for more time. Ignoring it can lead to a fine or a decision against you.

You may see it in letters from

  • Agencia Tributaria (AEAT) — Tax agency
  • Instituto Nacional de la Seguridad Social (INSS) — Social Security benefits office
  • Oficina de Extranjería / Policía Nacional — Immigration office
  • Dirección General de Tráfico (DGT) — Traffic authority
  • Agencia tributaria autonómica — Regional tax agency
  • Juzgado / Tribunal — Court
