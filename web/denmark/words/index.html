Danish office words from Denmark, explained

The words you see in official letters in Denmark, in plain English.

a-kasse
Unemployment insurance fund.
afdragsordning
A plan to pay a debt in monthly parts.
afgørelse
An official decision.
aktindsigt
Your right to see the documents in your case.
Ankestyrelsen
The national appeals board for many social decisions.
betalingsfrist
The last day to pay.
betalingspåkrav
A payment order from the court. You have 14 days to object.
biometri
Your photo and fingerprints, taken for a residence card.
boligstøtte
Housing benefit to help pay rent.
bødeforelæg
A fine notice. You can accept it and pay, or refuse so the case goes to court.
børne- og ungeydelse
Child benefit, paid four times a year.
CPR-nummer
Your personal identity number, like 300785-1234.
dagpenge
Unemployment benefit from your a-kasse.
depositum
Deposit you paid when you moved in.
Digital Post
The digital mailbox where Danish offices send letters. Letters there count as received.
FI-kort
A payment code like +71<123456789012345 +12345678<. You type it in your online bank to pay.
fogedretten
The bailiff’s court. It handles seizure for debts and evictions.
forskudsopgørelse
Your expected income and tax for the coming year. Keep it up to date to avoid restskat.
Gældsstyrelsen
The agency that collects debts to public bodies.
inkasso
Debt collection.
jobsamtale
A meeting at the job centre about your job search.
klage
A complaint against a decision.
klagevejledning
The part of a decision that tells you how, where and by when to complain.
kontanthjælp
Cash benefit from the kommune for people who can’t support themselves.
lønindeholdelse
Part of your salary is taken to pay a public debt.
MitID
Your digital ID for logging in to public and bank websites.
modregning
Money you should get, for example a tax refund, is used to pay a debt.
opholdstilladelse
Residence permit.
opsigelse
Notice that a contract will end.
overskydende skat
Tax you paid too much. You get it back.
partshøring
Your chance to give your side before a decision is made.
påkrav
A formal demand to pay unpaid rent. If you don’t pay in time, you can lose your home.
restskat
Tax you still have to pay for the year.
rykker
A payment reminder, often with a fee.
sanktion
A cut in your benefit, for example after a missed meeting.
Skattestyrelsen
The Danish tax office.
TastSelv
The self-service part of skat.dk where you see and change your tax.
Udbetaling Danmark
The office that pays many benefits, like child benefit, housing benefit and pension.
udlæg
Seizure of belongings or money for a debt.
årsopgørelse
Your yearly tax statement: money back or tax to pay.
