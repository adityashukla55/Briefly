Privacy

Your letter never leaves your device.

What happens to my letter?

Briefly reads, explains and translates your letter inside your browser. There is no server that receives it, and nothing is saved. Close the page and the letter is gone.

How can I be sure?

The page is locked by a security rule (Content Security Policy) that only allows connections to Briefly’s own website, to download the app itself. It cannot send data anywhere else. The code is open source, so anyone can check it.

What is downloaded?

The app, the text reader (about 6 MB) and, only if you use it, the translator for your language (20–26 MB). They are stored by your browser so Briefly works offline.

Cookies and tracking

None. Briefly only remembers which country you picked last, in your own browser.
