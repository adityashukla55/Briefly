What to do

  • If you can’t pay, you can ask CSN for a lower amount (nedsättning) — apply before the due date.
  • Find the deadline. Look for “senast”, “inom”, “sista dag”.
  • Write the reference number from the letter on everything you send back, and keep copies.
  • If you disagree with a decision, object in writing before the deadline (“omprövning”, “överklagande”, “bestridande”). See the template.

Questions

What is the CSN?

Student finance office. Pays student aid and collects student loan repayments.

How long do I have to answer a letter from the CSN?

It depends on the letter. Look for words like “senast”, “inom”, “sista dag” — they mark the deadline. In Sweden, deadlines to appeal usually start on the day you received the decision (fick del av beslutet). If the office used simplified delivery (förenklad delgivning), the letter counts as received two weeks after it was sent. Briefly adds about 2 days for post — check when your letter really arrived. To object, the letter usually names the way to do it (“omprövning”, “överklagande”, “bestridande”).
