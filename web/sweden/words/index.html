Swedish office words from Sweden, explained

The words you see in official letters in Sweden, in plain English.

a-kassa
Unemployment insurance fund, and the benefit it pays.
aktivitetsrapport
A monthly report to Arbetsförmedlingen about your job search.
anstånd
A delay — more time to pay or to answer.
avbetalningsplan
A plan to pay a debt in monthly parts.
bankgiro
An account number used for bill payments in Sweden, like 123-4567.
barnbidrag
Child benefit, paid every month for each child.
bestrida
To dispute a claim — to say you don’t agree that you owe the money.
betalningsanmärkning
A payment remark — a note that you didn’t pay a debt. It makes it hard to rent or borrow, usually for three years.
betalningsföreläggande
A payment order. Someone asks Kronofogden to decide that you must pay.
bostadsbidrag
Housing allowance for families with children and young people with low income.
ekonomiskt bistånd
Social assistance from the kommun for people who can’t support themselves.
fick del av beslutet
The day you received the decision. Many deadlines start then.
folkbokföring
The population register — where you are registered as living.
förfallodag
Due date — the last day to pay.
Försäkringskassan
The social insurance office. It pays child benefit, parental benefit, sickness benefit and more.
förvaltningsrätt
Administrative court. It handles appeals against decisions by offices and the kommun.
föräldrapenning
Parental benefit when you stay home with your child.
hyresnämnden
The rent tribunal. It helps settle disputes between tenants and landlords.
inkassokrav
A demand from a debt collector to pay an unpaid bill.
kallelse
An invitation to an appointment, meeting or hearing.
komplettering
A request to send more information or papers.
Kronofogden
The Swedish Enforcement Authority. It collects debts and handles payment orders.
OCR-nummer
Payment reference number. It links your payment to your bill.
omprövning
A request to the same office to look at its decision again.
ordningsbot
A fixed fine for a minor offence, for example speeding.
personnummer
Your personal identity number, like 850730-1234.
plusgiro
Another account number used for bill payments, like 12 34 56-7.
påminnelseavgift
A reminder fee. It is limited by law.
restskatt
Tax you still have to pay for the year.
samordningsnummer
An identity number for people who are not, or not yet, registered as living in Sweden.
skattekonto
Your tax account at Skatteverket. Tax payments and refunds go through it.
Skatteverket
The Swedish tax office. It also keeps the population register (folkbokföring).
skuldsanering
Debt relief decided by Kronofogden for people who can’t pay their debts.
slutskattebesked
Your final tax statement for the year: money back or tax to pay.
strafföreläggande
An offer from the prosecutor to accept a fine instead of a court case.
trängselskatt
Congestion tax for driving into central Stockholm or Gothenburg.
uppehållstillstånd
Residence permit.
uppsägning
Notice that a contract will end, for example your rental contract.
utmätning
Seizure of money or property to pay a debt, for example part of your salary.
återkrav
A claim to pay back money you got but weren’t entitled to.
överklaga
To appeal a decision to a court or higher office.
