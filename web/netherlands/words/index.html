Dutch office words from the Netherlands, explained

The words you see in official letters in the Netherlands, in plain English.

aanmaning
Formal payment demand. You paid late, and costs are often added.
aanslag
Tax assessment. A letter that says how much tax you must pay or get back.
afvalstoffenheffing
Waste tax. The gemeente charges it for collecting household rubbish. Tenants pay it too.
AOW
State pension. You build it up for each year you live or work in the Netherlands.
automatische incasso
Direct debit. They take the money from your bank account automatically.
Berichtenbox
Your digital mailbox in MijnOverheid. Offices can send official letters there instead of by post.
beroep
Appeal. Usually the next step after a bezwaar, at a court. In other letters, beroep can also mean ‘profession’.
beschikking
Official decision by an office about you, for example about a benefit or a permit. Also called besluit.
beslag
Seizure of money or goods to pay a debt, for example part of your wages.
beslagvrije voet
The part of your income that can’t be seized, so you can live on it.
betalingskenmerk
Payment reference. Type it in the payment reference field when you pay, exactly as written.
betalingsregeling
Payment plan. You pay a debt in monthly parts.
bezwaar
Objection. A letter to the office saying its decision is wrong, asking it to look again.
bijstand
Income support from your gemeente if you don’t have enough money to live on.
borg
Rental deposit. You get it back when you move out, minus costs for any damage.
BRP
The national register of residents (Basisregistratie Personen). Your gemeente records your address in it.
BSN
Citizen service number (burgerservicenummer). Your personal number for all government offices.
dagtekening
The date on the letter. The time to object usually starts the day after this date.
dagvaarding
Court summons. Someone has started a court case against you. It says when the case starts.
deurwaarder
Bailiff. A legal officer who delivers court papers and can seize money or goods.
DigiD
Your digital ID for logging in to government websites, like Mijn Belastingdienst or MijnOverheid.
dwangbevel
Enforcement order. With it, an office can collect a tax or fine by force, without going to court.
eigen risico
The part of your care costs you pay yourself each year before the insurance pays. Visits to your GP don’t count.
huurtoeslag
Rent allowance. It helps pay your rent if your income is low.
incassobureau
Debt collection agency. A company that collects unpaid bills for someone else.
IND
Immigration and Naturalisation Service. It decides on residence permits and Dutch citizenship.
inschrijving
Registration, for example of your address in the BRP. When you first register, you get a BSN.
jaarafrekening
Yearly bill. It compares what you used with the advance payments you made.
Juridisch Loket
Free legal advice service for people with a low or middle income.
kinderbijslag
Child benefit. Money from the SVB to help with the costs of children under 18.
kwijtschelding
Cancellation of a tax or debt because your income is too low to pay it.
machtiging
Authorisation. Permission for someone to act for you, or for a company to take money from your account.
MijnOverheid
The government’s personal website. You see your data and your digital post (Berichtenbox) there.
opzegtermijn
Notice period. The time between giving notice and the end of a contract.
OZB
Property tax (onroerendezaakbelasting). Owners of a home pay it to the gemeente.
schuldhulpverlening
Free debt help from your gemeente. They help you get an overview and make a plan with the people you owe.
servicekosten
Service charges paid with the rent, for example for cleaning or lighting in shared areas.
termijn
Time limit or deadline. In payment letters, a termijn can also be one instalment.
terugvordering
Claim to pay money back, because you got too much.
toeslag
Allowance. Money from the government to help with costs like health insurance, rent or childcare.
uitstel van betaling
More time to pay.
UWV
Employee insurance agency. It pays unemployment, sickness and disability benefits.
verblijfsvergunning
Residence permit. It lets you live in the Netherlands for a reason, like work, study or family.
voorlopige aanslag
Provisional tax assessment. An advance tax bill or refund based on your expected income.
voorschot
Advance payment. Money paid before the final amount is known, for example for energy or a toeslag.
waterschapsbelasting
Water board tax. It pays for dikes, water levels and cleaning waste water.
WW
Unemployment benefit (Werkloosheidswet). You may get it for a while if you lose your job.
zienswijze
Your view. Your chance to react in writing before the office makes its decision.
zorgtoeslag
Health care allowance. It helps pay your health insurance if your income is low.
zorgverzekering
Health insurance. Everyone who lives or works in the Netherlands must have basic health insurance (basisverzekering).
