German office words from Austria, explained

The words you see in official letters in Austria, in plain English.

Abgabennachforderung
Extra tax to pay. You paid less than you owe.
Anmeldebescheinigung
Registration certificate for EU citizens who stay longer than 3 months. Apply within 4 months after you arrive.
Anonymverfügung
Fine sent to the car owner when the driver is not known. You can’t object to it.
Arbeitnehmerveranlagung
Yearly tax return for employees. Many people get money back, and you can file it up to 5 years later.
Arbeitslosengeld
Unemployment benefit from the AMS.
Aufenthaltstitel
Residence permit.
Aussetzung der Einhebung
A pause in collecting a disputed tax until your objection is decided. If you lose, interest may be added.
Bescheid
Official decision. It becomes final if you don’t object in time.
Beschwerde
Objection to an official decision (Bescheid). Usually within 4 weeks; for tax decisions within one month.
Betriebskosten
Shared building costs on top of the rent, like water, rubbish and cleaning.
e-card
Your health insurance card. Bring it to every doctor’s visit.
Einspruch
Objection, for example to a fine (Strafverfügung, within 2 weeks) or to a court payment order (Zahlungsbefehl).
Exekution
Enforcement. A creditor takes money or things from you with official permission, for example part of your wages.
Fälligkeitstag
Due date. Pay by this day.
Familienbeihilfe
Monthly child benefit, paid by the tax office (Finanzamt Österreich).
Feber
February (an Austrian word).
FinanzOnline
The tax office’s online portal. Letters can arrive in its Databox instead of by post.
Frist
Deadline.
Gutschrift
Credit. Money you get back or that is added to your account.
Hauptwohnsitz
Your main home — the place where you mainly live.
Hinterlegung
The post office keeps an official letter for you because you were not home. You get a notice slip.
Jänner
January (the Austrian word).
Kontrollmeldung
Required appointment at the AMS.
Lenkerauskunft
Request to the car owner to say who was driving.
Mahnspesen
Fee added for a payment reminder.
Mandatsbescheid
Quick decision made without hearing you first. You can object (Vorstellung) within 2 weeks.
Meldezettel
Form to register your address. You must register within 3 days after moving in.
Notstandshilfe
Support from the AMS after your unemployment benefit ends, if you still need it.
ORF-Beitrag
Fee for public TV and radio. One payment per household with a main home in Austria, even without a TV.
Organmandat
Small on-the-spot fine, for example from the police or a parking warden.
Parteiengehör
Your right to see the evidence and comment before they decide.
Pfändung
Seizure of money or things to pay a debt. A basic amount to live on (Existenzminimum) stays protected.
Ratenzahlung
Paying in instalments.
Rechtsmittelbelehrung
The part at the end that explains how and by when you can object.
Rot-Weiß-Rot-Karte
Residence and work permit for skilled workers from outside the EU.
RSa / RSb
Registered official letter that you must sign for. An RSa letter is only given to you; an RSb letter can also be given to another adult at your address.
Säumniszuschlag
Late payment charge, for example when you pay tax too late.
Schuldanerkenntnis
A signed statement that you owe the money. Don’t sign it before you have checked the debt.
Sozialhilfe / Mindestsicherung
Basic support to live on if your income is too low. It is paid by your province; in Vienna it is called Mindestsicherung.
Sozialversicherungsnummer
Your 10-digit social insurance number. It is on your e-card.
Straferkenntnis
Fine decision after a full procedure. You can object (Beschwerde) within 4 weeks.
Strafverfügung
Fine from the police or district authority. You can object (Einspruch) within 2 weeks after delivery.
Stundung
Permission to pay later.
Verbesserungsauftrag
Request to fix or complete your application by a deadline. If you don’t, they can reject it.
Verwaltungsübertretung
A minor offence, for example a traffic or parking offence. It is not a crime.
Vorstellung
Objection to a quick decision (Mandatsbescheid) that was made without hearing you first. Usually within 2 weeks.
Zahlungsbefehl
Court order to pay. If you don’t owe the money, object (Einspruch) in time — usually within 4 weeks.
Zahlungserleichterung
Request to pay later or in instalments, for example at the tax office. Ask before the due date.
Zustellung
Formal delivery of a letter. Most deadlines start on the day of delivery.
