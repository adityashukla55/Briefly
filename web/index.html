Briefly

Got an official letter?
Understand it in a minute.

Take a photo or open a PDF of a letter from an office, landlord or company. Briefly finds the deadline, the amount and what you need to do — in plain English.

  • No account Just open it and start. No sign-up, no email.
  • No upload Your letter never leaves your device.
  • Works offline Even without internet, after your first visit.

Where is the letter from?

Drop your letter here

Photo (JPG, PNG) or PDF. You can add several pages.

How? Briefly has no server. Everything runs inside your browser, and the page is locked so it can’t send your letter anywhere.

No letter at hand? Try an example:

  1. Read. Text recognition runs right here in your browser.
  2. Understand. Briefly knows the offices, deadlines and rules of each country, and finds who wrote, what they want and the money.
  3. Act. A checklist, a calendar reminder and ready-made German replies.

What works

Works

  • Photos (JPG, PNG, WebP) and PDFs, up to 8 pages
  • Typed letters from Germany, Austria, Switzerland (German), France, Spain and Italy
  • The latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones and computers
  • Offline, after your first visit

Doesn’t work (yet)

  • Handwritten letters, or letters from other countries
  • Password-protected PDFs
  • HEIC photos outside Safari — take a screenshot instead
  • Old browsers that haven’t been updated for years

The first time you read a photo, Briefly downloads its text reader (about 6 MB). The translator for each language is an extra 20–26 MB, only if you use it. Everything stays on your device.
