What works

Works

  • Photos (JPG, PNG, WebP) and PDFs, up to 8 pages
  • Typed official letters from the countries above
  • The latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones and computers
  • Offline, after your first visit

Doesn’t work (yet)

  • Handwritten letters, or letters from other countries
  • Password-protected PDFs
  • HEIC photos outside Safari — take a screenshot instead
  • Old browsers that haven’t been updated for years

After your first visit, Briefly saves its text reader for your country (about 6 MB), so reading letters works offline. The translator is an extra 20–26 MB per language — save it under “Offline use” below. English letters need no translator. Everything stays on your device.
