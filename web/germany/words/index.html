German office words from Germany, explained

The words you see in official letters in Germany, in plain English.

Ablehnung
Rejection.
Aktenzeichen
Case number. Write it on every reply.
Anhörung
Your chance to explain your side before they decide.
Anmeldung
Registering your address with the city.
Antrag
Application.
Aufenthaltstitel
Residence permit.
Bedarfsgemeinschaft
Everyone in your household who is counted together for benefits.
Beitragsnummer
Your account number for the TV and radio fee.
Bekanntgabe
The day the letter counts as delivered. For posted letters, usually 4 days after the date on the letter.
Bescheid
Official decision. It becomes final if you don’t object in time.
Bewilligung
Approval.
Bußgeld
Fine.
Einspruch
Objection. Used for tax offices, fines and court orders. It must be sent before the deadline.
Erstattung
Refund — or, in some letters, a demand that you pay money back. Check who pays whom.
Fiktionsbescheinigung
Temporary paper that lets you stay legally while your permit is being processed.
Forderung
Claim. Money someone says you owe.
Frist
Deadline.
Guthaben
Credit. Money that belongs to you.
Inkasso
Debt collection.
Kassenzeichen
Payment reference. Put it in the reference field when you pay.
Kaution
Rental deposit.
Klage
Lawsuit. Going to court.
Kündigung
Ending a contract.
Lastschrift
Direct debit. They take the money from your account automatically.
Leistungen
Benefits — money or services you get from the state.
Leistungsminderung
Cut in your benefits, for example after a missed appointment.
Mahngebühr
Late fee added because of a reminder.
Mahnung
Payment reminder. You paid late or not at all.
Meldetermin
Required appointment at the Jobcenter or employment agency.
Mitwirkungspflicht
Your duty to help, for example by sending documents. If you don’t, they can stop payments.
Nachweis
Proof, for example a payslip, contract or certificate.
Nachzahlung
Extra payment. You owe more than you already paid.
Nebenkosten
Extra costs on top of the rent, like heating and water.
Pfändung
Seizure of money or things to pay a debt.
Ratenzahlung
Paying in instalments.
Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung
The part at the end that explains how and by when you can object.
Rundfunkbeitrag
Public TV and radio fee. One per household.
Säumniszuschlag
Late payment charge, added for every month you are late.
Stellungnahme
Your written statement or explanation.
Steuer-ID
Your personal 11-digit tax ID. It never changes.
Stundung
Postponing a payment.
Überweisung
Bank transfer.
Unterlagen
Documents or papers.
unverzüglich
Immediately, without delay.
Verwarnungsgeld
Small fine, up to 55 €.
Verwendungszweck
The reference text for a bank transfer.
Vollstreckung
Enforcement. Taking money by force, for example from your bank account.
Widerspruch
Objection. A written complaint that a decision is wrong. It must be sent before the deadline.
Zustellung
Formal delivery. The delivery date is often written on the envelope — keep it.
