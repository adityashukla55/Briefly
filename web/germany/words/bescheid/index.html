What does “Bescheid” mean?

Official decision. It becomes final if you don’t object in time.

What to do

Look at the end of the letter (Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung): it says how long you have to object.

You may see it in letters from

  • Finanzamt — Tax office
  • Hauptzollamt — Customs office
  • Jobcenter — Jobcenter
  • Agentur für Arbeit — Employment agency
  • ARD ZDF Deutschlandradio Beitragsservice — Broadcasting fee office
  • Krankenkasse — Health insurance
