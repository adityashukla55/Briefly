Portuguese office words from Portugal, explained

The words you see in official letters in Portugal, in plain English.

abono de família
Child benefit.
agente de execução
An enforcement agent who collects court debts, for example by seizing salary.
apoio judiciário
Legal aid. Segurança Social can pay for a lawyer and court costs if your income is low.
audiência prévia
Your right to give your side before a final decision.
auto de contraordenação
An offence notice, for example for speeding or parking.
autorização de residência
Residence permit.
carta registada
Registered letter. You get proof that it was sent.
citação
Official notice that a court case or enforcement was started against you. Deadlines start from it.
coima
A fine for an administrative offence, for example speeding or a late tax return.
contestação
Your written defence in a court case.
convocatória
An official invitation to an appointment.
deferimento
Approval of your request.
defesa
Your written explanation against a fine or accusation.
despejo
Eviction.
dias úteis
Working days — weekends and public holidays don’t count.
execução fiscal
Forced collection of unpaid tax by the tax office, for example by seizing salary or bank money.
Finanças
The tax office (Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira, AT).
IMI
Yearly property tax for the owner of a house or flat.
impugnação judicial
Challenging a tax bill in court. Usually within 3 months.
indeferimento
Refusal of your request.
injunção
A quick court procedure for a debt. You have 15 days to pay or oppose.
IRS
Personal income tax (imposto sobre o rendimento das pessoas singulares).
IUC
Yearly car tax, paid in the month the car was first registered.
Multibanco
The Portuguese cash machine and payment network. You pay bills with an entity number (entidade) and a reference (referência).
NIF
Tax identification number (número de identificação fiscal). A 9-digit personal number you need for almost everything in Portugal.
NISS
Social security number (número de identificação da segurança social).
nota de cobrança
A tax bill with the amount, the deadline and a payment reference.
nota de liquidação
The tax office’s calculation of your tax: a refund or tax to pay.
notificação
An official letter that tells you about a decision or a step in a process.
oposição
A formal objection, for example against an enforcement or an injunção.
pagamento em prestações
Paying a debt in monthly parts.
pagamento voluntário
Paying a fine early, usually at the minimum amount.
penhora
Seizure of money or goods to pay a debt — for example part of your salary.
Portal das Finanças
The tax office website. You can see letters, pay and object there.
prazo
Deadline or time limit.
prescrição
When a debt or fine is too old to be collected.
reclamação graciosa
A free complaint to the tax office against a tax bill.
recurso hierárquico
An appeal to a higher level of the same office.
referência para pagamento
The number you use to pay a bill at a Multibanco machine, in home banking or online.
renda
Rent.
restituição
Paying back money you received but weren’t entitled to.
Segurança Social Direta
The social security website, where you can see letters, apply and object.
senhorio
Landlord.
subsídio de desemprego
Unemployment benefit.
