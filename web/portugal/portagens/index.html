What to do

  • Pay toll notices quickly — costs grow and can end up as a tax debt.
  • Find the deadline. Look for “prazo”, “até”, “data limite”.
  • Write the reference number from the letter on everything you send back, and keep copies.

Questions

What is the Portagens (Via Verde / Infraestruturas de Portugal)?

Toll roads. Unpaid road tolls. If you don’t pay, extra costs are added and the tax office can collect them.

How long do I have to answer a letter from the Portagens (Via Verde / Infraestruturas de Portugal)?

It depends on the letter. Look for words like “prazo”, “até”, “data limite” — they mark the deadline. In Portugal, a letter sent by registered post is usually treated as received on the third day after it was posted. Check if the deadline is in working days (dias úteis) or in calendar days. To object, the letter usually names the way to do it (“reclamação graciosa”, “audiência prévia”, “defesa”, “oposição”, “impugnação”, “recurso”, “reclamação”).
